# Privacy Policy – Stremio GDrive Addon Last updated: November 6, 2026 ## Overview Stremio GDrive Addon is a personal-use application that allows users to access Google Drive content through Stremio. ## Google Drive Access The application uses Google OAuth to authorize access to Google Drive. Access is limited to the permissions requested during authentication. ## Data Collection The application does not intentionally collect personal information beyond what is necessary to provide its functionality. Google account authentication and Drive access are handled through Google's authorization system. ## Data Storage Any credentials or tokens stored by the application are used to maintain authentication and provide the service. Users should secure their deployment and avoid sharing credentials. ## Third-Party Services This application relies on Google Drive and Google OAuth services, which are subject to Google's privacy policy. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## Contact For privacy-related questions, contact the application administrator.
